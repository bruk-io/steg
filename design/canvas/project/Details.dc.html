Terminal themes and the focus ring

The terminal's colours are kept apart from the window's, so a theme can be swapped without touching the chrome. Red, green, yellow and magenta are the ramp's red, green, amber and pink, and blue is the session blue; cyan is the one colour of its own. Programs choose among the sixteen, so steg cannot promise the contrast of every pair. Use Tweaks to see this board in light, or in another session colour.

A 2px ring in the session colour, 2px clear of the control, shown only when focus arrives from the keyboard. It is the one line the design draws in normal use. Its 12px corner follows the control's 8px corner at that distance.
