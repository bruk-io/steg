Layout and resizing

The handful of arrangements every part of steg is built from, and the one rule for what happens when room runs out. Values such as gaps and radii are on the first Foundations board; this board says how they combine. Use Tweaks to see this board in light, or in another session colour.

Resizing

The one rule every component follows when room runs out. Each card that can resize has a Sizes block that fills this in.

Rules
  • A component shrinks by dropping whole parts in a stated order. It never squeezes, fades or overlaps them.
  • An icon and a status are never dropped and never cut off. Text is the first thing to shorten.
  • What is dropped stays reachable: a label becomes the tooltip, a status stays on the ring in the bar.
  • Parts of one kind change together: every chip shortens at once, every tab in a track is the same width.
